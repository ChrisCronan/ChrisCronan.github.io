About

I started as an engineer. I still lead like one.

Find the real problem. Clear what’s in the way. Make the next step obvious. It works on products and programs. It works on people, too.

I’ve been leading people for more than 25 years, in the Marine Corps, on the factory floor, and across engineering programs. Today I’m an NPI & Technical Program Portfolio Leader at Tesla. Before that, I led new technology and new model programs at Honda, and learned the work from the ground up as a robotics and field service engineer at Stäubli and DNS Electronics.

I served in the U.S. Marine Corps from 2003 to 2008, and I hold an MS in Engineering Management from Ohio University.

Different places. Same lesson: the work gets better when the people doing it get better.

What I believe

  • Clarity beats charisma. People follow what they understand.
  • Small habits outlast big speeches. Leadership shows up on a Tuesday, not just in a town hall.
  • Grow people, not dependence. The best leaders make themselves a little less necessary every year.
  • Calm is contagious. So is panic. Choose which one you spread.
